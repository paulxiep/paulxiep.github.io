I personally believe Anthropic and OpenAI will go out of business.

Not in a tautological all-things-end way, I believe sooner than that.

The trigger is when they have to deal with post-subsidy operational realities.


3 things can be true at the same time.
1. Anthropic and OpenAI bubble will burst.
2. They have contributed to era-defining technological advancements.
3. In so doing, they also created societal upheaval and exacerbated inequality.


Some argue AI is a democratisation of intelligence.

I believe "All people have equal access. Some people have more equal access than others."

Now, the core point.

I believe most VC-backed startups fail eventually, because the economic of their products simply doesn't work.

Why VC still invest - it's portfolio management. They make a 100 small bets so that one successful bet pays for all the others and more.

Now, the 2 frontier AI labs, from their own financial statements, I simply don't see how their economic is close to working.

Small to mid-sized businesses and operators already avoid paying for API rate.

Revolut recently switched to self-hosting.

I believe that's a precedent most businesses will follow.

Businesses with scale will host their own. Small-to-mid will find open model provider that's sustainable.


VC might have accelerated advancements.

The frontier labs will still go out of business.
